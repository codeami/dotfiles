# 04-retrieve — question → the right note(s)

What do I do: given a question or pain, find the right context at the right time.
Read shared/index.md first, follow links, return only the notes that answer the question — with their evidence.

## Inputs
| Source | File/Location | Scope | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| User | the question | full | what to answer |
| Reference | ../../shared/index.md | full | the map |

## Outputs
Return: the selected note(s), with claim + evidence. Do not write new files.

## Rules
- The bottleneck is the right thinking at the right time — not more notes.
- If nothing answers, say so (a signal for 05-review, not a reason to write a new note).
