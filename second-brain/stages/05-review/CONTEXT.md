# 05-review — audit the brain

What do I do: audit for decay. Find orphaned notes (not in index), stale claims (evidence superseded),
contradictions, and overgrown notes (more than one idea). Propose edits; do not apply destructive ones without approval.

## Inputs
| Source | File/Location | Scope | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reference | ../../shared/index.md | full | the graph |
| Reference | ../../nodes/ | all | the notes |

## Outputs
Write: output/review.md — findings + proposed edits.

## Rules
- Report what changed, what is stale, what is open. Do not hide a gap.
- Destructive edits (delete, rewrite) need approval.
