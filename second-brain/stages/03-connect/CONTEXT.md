# 03-connect — note → the graph

What do I do: place the note in the graph. Add it to shared/index.md (one line: link + one-line claim),
and add tags and [[links]] to the note itself. Structure decides what matters.

## Inputs
| Source | File/Location | Scope | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Previous stage | nodes/<slug>.md | the note | the thing to connect |
| Reference | ../../shared/index.md | current graph | where it lands |

## Outputs
Edit: shared/index.md (add one entry under its tag)
Edit: nodes/<slug>.md (add links to related notes)

## Rules
- Every note appears in the index. No orphans.
- Link to at least one related note or tag.
