# 02-distill — raw capture → atomic note

What do I do: turn one inbox capture into one atomic note in nodes/. One idea per note.
Every note has a claim, verbatim evidence, and a source link. If a capture holds several ideas, make several notes.

## Inputs
| Source | File/Location | Scope | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Previous stage | inbox/<slug>.md | the capture | the signal to distill |

## Outputs
Write: nodes/<slug>.md
Format: tags, links, source, claim (one line), evidence (verbatim), elaboration.

## Rules
- One idea per note. Split, never merge.
- claim + verbatim evidence + source are mandatory.
- No unsourced assertions.
