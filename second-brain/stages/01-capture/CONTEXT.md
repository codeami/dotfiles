# 01-capture — dialogue → raw capture

What do I do: turn a dialogue, transcript, or source into a short raw capture in inbox/.
Keep the short file — drop greetings, fluff, and repetition. Preserve verbatim quotes you might need as evidence.

## Inputs
| Source | File/Location | Scope | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| User | dialogue / transcript (pasted or referenced) | full | the raw signal |

## Outputs
Write: inbox/<slug>.md
Format: source, date, a compact transcript of the signal, and any verbatim quotes worth keeping.

## Rules
- Keep only the signal. Short is the point.
- Preserve verbatim quotes; they become evidence in 02-distill.
- Do not distill yet. Do not link yet. One job.
