# The captain's mindset

Claim: Once orchestration absorbs the work, the bottleneck shifts to direction — talk to users, read the landscape, and craft the treasure map.

Evidence: "you start to run out of ideas for what to ask... focusing more of your energy on understanding what matters... crafting a good treasure map."

Elaboration:
- Running out of tasks to hand out is a signal the bottleneck has moved, not a problem.
- The captain's job is direction, not whack-a-mole across crewmates.
- The same shift applies to the second brain: once the loop runs, the human curates the map.
