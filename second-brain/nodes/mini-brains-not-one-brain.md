# Mini brains, not one brain of everything

Claim: Build many small, per-workflow brains, each bespoke to one high-value operation, instead of one brain of everything.

Evidence: "I actually make multiple second brains based on workflows, not based on everything I'm doing."

Elaboration:
- A high-tea brain is a second brain purely around how the talks are made.
- Once it is happy, it can be tossed into any other ICM or turned into a template.
- "This second brain is my high value operation."
