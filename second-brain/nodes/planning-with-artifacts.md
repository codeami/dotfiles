# Plan against artifacts, not text

Claim: For complex work, plan against an interactive HTML artifact (Lavish) rather than a wall of text, so feedback points at specific elements.

Evidence: "I can also annotate and make comments on specific parts of the artifacts... Honestly, I can never go back to reading text in the terminal anymore."

Elaboration:
- Lavish renders options in the project's own design system, making concepts concrete and prototypes visible.
- Annotate elements, edit diagrams, decide at the bottom, and send feedback without leaving the artifact.
- The wall of text cannot be pointed at; the artifact can.
