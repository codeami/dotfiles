# The bottleneck: right thinking at the right time

Claim: The model's bottleneck is not capability but retrieval — knowing which knowledge to surface and when.

Evidence: "The bottleneck these models have is the thinking of the right things at the right time. Where should the knowledge be?"

Elaboration:
- To know where knowledge goes, first know how you do the work manually.
- The structure exists so the model finds the right file fast, not so it generates more files.
- Barely any tokens and "almost no seconds" when the graph routes correctly (the finance-team example).
