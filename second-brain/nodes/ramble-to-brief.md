# Ramble to brief

Claim: Kick off a project by rambling your full thinking at the agent and letting it rationalize the requirements into a brief.

Evidence: "3,000 characters is a lot... this is typically how I kick off a new project. I just ramble, I try to explain everything I have in mind to first mate and let the agent figure things out."

Elaboration:
- The captain dumps requirements, ideas, and open questions; the agent structures them.
- The ramble becomes the brief, the market research, and the technical plan.
- Voice input makes the ramble cheap; the agent makes it legible.
