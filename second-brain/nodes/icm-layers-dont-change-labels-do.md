# ICM: the layers don't change, the labels do

Claim: Every workspace has the same three layers; only the names, the context files and the routing rows change with the kind of work. Choose 2–4 workspaces by the modes you shift between, and treat "I wish the agent would forget what it was just doing" as the signal for a workspace boundary.

Evidence: Three worked structures — content creator (script-lab / production / distribution), consultant (one folder per client + templates + business-dev, rule: never reference one client's information in another's workspace), developer (planning / src / docs / ops with a Skills column). "When you onboard a new client, you copy the structure, write a new CONTEXT.md… the CLAUDE.md at the top gets one new line in the routing table." 4.5: two jobs from the same meeting notes need two rooms and a deliberate choice about what can be shared.

Elaboration:
- Keep each CONTEXT.md under a page; they are working notes, not finished documents.
- Naming conventions in the map (`topic_draft.md`, `YYYY-MM-platform-topic.md`, `YYYY-MM-DD-decision-title.md`) replace a database for small systems.
- Separate folders do not enforce isolation; permissions do.

Why it matters here: `areas/` is the room list (one folder per kind of work) — and FirstMate worktrees are the "forget what you were just doing" boundary made physical.
