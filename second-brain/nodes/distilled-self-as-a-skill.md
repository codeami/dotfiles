# A distilled self is a skill

Claim: A person's judgment can be distilled into a continuously-updated skill, so agents can act with that judgment instead of escalating every decision.

Evidence: "I distilled myself into a skill... I set up an automation to continuously update the distillation based on what I publicly said, wrote, and built."

Elaboration:
- Kun's bottleneck: agents "constantly bottlenecked on my judgment... They escalate decisions to me when they aren't sure."
- "A distillation is by definition a subset... My moat is in the way I go about acquiring more and more knowledge."
- Freeing repeatable, non-moat work leaves time to strengthen the real moat.
- Method: distill from public speech, writing, and code; the same process works on others (Altman, Musk, Amodei, Zuckerberg were distilled into debate bots).
