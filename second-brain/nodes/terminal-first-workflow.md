# Terminal-first workflow

Claim: A terminal-first setup keeps hands on the keyboard (staying in flow) and gives the same workflow everywhere, even a phone.

Evidence: "to allow my hands to almost never have to leave the keyboard... when your hands stay on the keyboard, you stay in the flow." / "I can keep the exact same workflow everywhere, even on my phone."

Elaboration:
- Mouse use forces a context switch; terminal apps are keyboard-native by design.
- tmux persistence is the enabler: detach and resume the same sessions from another device.
- WezTerm + tmux + Neovim are the ship; the agent harnesses are the crew.
