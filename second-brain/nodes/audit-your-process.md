# Audit your own process

Claim: Find where structure should go by asking concrete audit questions about your own process, then map answers to folders and skills.

Evidence: "What processes produce the most value for you? Which processes are the most annoying?... What tools do I use for those processes? When do I use them?"

Elaboration:
- Also ask: when do I not like to use AI? Where am I worried (sensitive, accountability)?
- Answers map to structure: a data source needs a folder; math needs a skill with a script; notes need a place.
- This is the 05-review stage made concrete — auditing is how the brain stays alive.
