# Onboarding — start from a pain, not a plan

Answer these once. They configure the factory (Layer 3), not a product.

1. What is the recurring pain? (the moment you think "I have solved this before")
2. What signal must survive? (the 20% of every capture that matters)
3. What noise must be dropped? (what turns notes into a graveyard)
4. Who reads this — just you, or you + agents + a team?
5. What is the first real thing to capture? (one dialogue, one session)

Write the answers here, then distill the answers themselves into _config/principles.md.
